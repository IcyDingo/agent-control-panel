Get the worker

One line in Terminal. It installs a small background service that counts your agents' work and keeps your galaxy here up to date. You need a Mac on macOS 14 or newer and Node 20 or newer. Claude Code does the rest; with Apple's Command Line Tools your commits count too.

          Line Tools. The installer says what is missing.
